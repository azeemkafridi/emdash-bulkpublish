1. Install the plugin and approve what it asks for: reading your published
   posts and their images, and sending them to `app.bulkpublish.com`.
2. In BulkPublish, open **Settings → API keys** and create a key.
3. In EmDash, open **BulkPublish** in the sidebar, paste the key and save.
4. Tick the channels to share to, check the caption, and save again.

From then on, every post you publish in the chosen collections is shared.
