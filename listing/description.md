Share every post you publish to your social accounts, without leaving EmDash.

When a post goes live, BulkPublish shares it to the channels you pick: Instagram,
Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky, Pinterest, Mastodon, Telegram, Discord,
Tumblr, Google Business Profile, Snapchat and more.

- **Automatic.** Publish as usual. Each post is shared once, so editing a live
  post never posts it again.
- **Your caption.** A template with `{title}`, `{excerpt}` and `{url}`. Posts
  too long for X, Bluesky or Threads are shortened for those networks, and the
  link is always kept whole.
- **Featured image included.** The post's image goes with it, so image-first
  networks like Instagram and Pinterest get a proper post. Posts without an
  image skip those networks and tell you why.
- **See what happened.** The editor's Share to social panel shows where a post
  went, what was skipped and why, and lets you share it again.
