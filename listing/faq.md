**Do I need a BulkPublish account?**
Yes. Connect your social accounts there first; this plugin shares to them.

**Will editing a published post share it again?**
No. Each post is shared once. Use **Share again** in the post's Share to social
panel to share it a second time.

**Why was Instagram skipped?**
Instagram, Pinterest and Snapchat need an image. Add a featured image to the
post, or leave those channels off. TikTok and YouTube only take videos, so blog
posts are never sent there.

**Can I share pages or another collection?**
Yes. List the collections under **Collections to share** in settings.

**What does the plugin send?**
The post's title, excerpt, public link and featured image, to your BulkPublish
account only.
